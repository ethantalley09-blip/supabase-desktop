Dana Reyes for State Senate — Field Program Impact Reference (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)
Updated monthly by the field director

NOTE: This document is fictional example data written for demo/testing
purposes. Any real campaign should replace these figures with its own
actual, current vendor rates and program costs before using them in real
donor communications — quoting a made-up figure to a real donor would be
dishonest.

Approximate current program costs, used to translate a dollar amount into
a concrete, specific description of what it funds (never round up, never
imply a donation funds more than its actual proportional share):

- $1 funds approximately 10 peer-to-peer text messages to likely voters.
- $5 funds one canvasser door-knock shift-hour in a targeted precinct.
- $10 funds about 50 personalized reminder postcards.
- $25 funds one hour of relational-organizing phone banking.
- $50 funds roughly 500 text messages to undecided voters in the district.
- $100 funds a half-day canvassing shift covering about 40 doors.
- $250 funds a full precinct captain's supply kit for one weekend.
- $500 funds a local cable TV ad spot in one media market for one day.

These are approximate, rounded figures based on the campaign's own recent
vendor invoices, not guarantees tied to any specific future purchase.
Field program overhead (roughly 15% of the field budget) covers staff
time, data/software costs, and printing, and is not represented in the
per-dollar figures above.
