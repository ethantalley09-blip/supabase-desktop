# AI features — a guide for campaign staff

Lynx has a built-in AI assistant that works entirely off **your own campaign
data** (your voters, territories, donations, and notes). It never sends
individual voter or donor records anywhere — only anonymous totals — and it will
not invent facts, endorsements, or numbers.

## Turning it on

Two things are needed, both one-time:

1. An administrator enables the **AI module** for your organization.
2. Your role has the **Use AI** permission — Owner, Manager, Media, Fundraiser,
   and Canvasser roles have it by default.

Once both are set, an **AI Center** tab appears on each project, and small AI
buttons appear next to messages, imports, and the fundraising tools. If you
don't see them, ask your admin to enable the AI module for your org.

> Behind the scenes an administrator also sets an API key on the server. Until
> that's done, AI buttons show a short "AI isn't configured" message instead of
> failing silently.

## Your tools are curated to your role

The AI Center opens with a **"Your AI tools"** panel showing exactly which
tools your role unlocks — a Canvasser sees turf and comms tools; a Fundraiser
sees the full fundraising suite; an Owner sees everything. Nobody is shown
tools irrelevant to their job, and nobody needs to hunt for what's available —
it's listed right at the top, grouped by category.

## What you can do

### AI Center (the hub)

- **Ask your data** — type a question in plain English ("How many ballots are
  still outstanding?", "Which city has the most voters?", "How close are we to
  the compliance threshold?") and get a direct answer. No filters or reports to
  build.
- **Smart Segments** — describe the voters you want ("Spanish-speaking voters in
  Ward 3 with an outstanding ballot") and get the actual list back, then turn it
  into a walk list with one click.
- **Campaign Coach** — click **Plan my day** to get your three highest-impact
  next actions, each with why it matters and where to do it.
- **Message Studio** — draft a team broadcast, canvassing script, or personal
  text. Pick a tone, describe it, and get copy you can edit, **Refine** (one tap:
  Shorter, Warmer, More urgent, Simpler, Fix grammar), copy, or translate.
- **Content Pack** — one brief becomes a coordinated email, text, canvassing
  script, and social post, each ready to copy or translate.

### Turf map
- **Ask your voters** and **Field Coach** right on the map.
- **Note digest** summarizes canvass notes into themes and follow-ups.
- **Suggest with AI** in the voter import auto-maps messy spreadsheet columns.

### Comms
- **Draft with AI** when composing a broadcast or a reply.
- **Translate** under every message in a thread.

### Outreach
- **Outreach Booster** drafts a personal text for a supporter to send a friend,
  plus a "save our number" contact card so it isn't filtered as spam.

### Fundraising — the full AI suite
- **Donor Insights** — flags your top network "connectors" and donors at risk
  of lapsing.
- **Ask Optimizer** — predicts the right ask amount for each donor (not a
  generic $25/$50/$100).
- **Major Donor Ladder** — flags mid-tier donors ready for a personal
  major-gift ask, with a draft you send yourself.
- **Momentum Detector** — checks your real donation pace against your own
  baseline and drafts a rapid-response ask during a genuine spike (never fake
  urgency).
- **Payment Recovery** — drafts a warm, no-shame note when a recurring
  donor's card fails, so that revenue isn't silently lost.
- **Volunteer-to-Donor Bridge** — invites your active volunteers who've never
  donated to also give, citing their real field contribution.
- **Recurring Upgrade** — an anniversary-style ask to raise a long-time
  recurring donor's monthly gift.
- **FEC Sprint Planner** — a day-by-day plan to hit a filing-deadline goal.
- **Retention Sequence** — a timed thank-you, impact update, and soft second
  ask after every gift.
- **Fatigue Guard** — checks send frequency before your next blast, so you
  don't burn out your list.
- **Copy Variation Tester** — generates FEC-compliant A/B message variants
  (never invents deadlines or matching-gift offers).
- **Donor Message Studio** — thank-you notes and donation asks, honest and
  pressure-free.
- **LTV Forecast** — flags which small, early donors are worth cultivating.
- **Donor Identity Resolution** — suggests when two donor records (e.g. from
  different import sources) are the same person. Always a suggestion — a human
  reviews and approves before anything merges.
- **Refund Watchdog** — an early warning if your refund/chargeback rate spikes
  against your own baseline.

### Translation
Under any AI-generated message, pick a language and click **Translate**. Nine
common languages are supported, and where your voter file lists a preferred
language, the translator defaults to your electorate's most common one.

## Good to know

- **It only uses your data.** Answers and coaching are based on aggregate counts
  from this project. It cannot see another organization's data.
- **It won't make things up.** If the data can't answer a question, it says so.
  Drafts won't invent endorsements, poll numbers, or matching-gift offers.
- **Compliance is not automated.** Lynx deliberately does not use AI to
  interpret campaign-finance or election law — always rely on counsel for that.
- **Nothing merges or sends itself.** Donor-identity suggestions, drafted
  messages, and asks always wait for a human to review and act.
- **Always review before sending.** AI drafts are a starting point; read them,
  edit for your voice and the facts, and you stay in control.
