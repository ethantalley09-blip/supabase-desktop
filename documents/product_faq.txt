Aurora Robotics — SkyNav Product FAQ

What is SkyNav?
SkyNav is Aurora Robotics' autonomous drone navigation platform. It ships
as an SDK that integrates with third-party drone hardware to provide
GPS-denied indoor navigation using visual-inertial odometry.

What pricing tiers are available?
- Starter: $199/month, up to 5 drones, community support only.
- Professional: $899/month, up to 50 drones, includes 24/5 email support
  and a dedicated Slack channel.
- Enterprise: custom pricing, unlimited drones, 24/7 phone support with a
  30-minute response SLA, and an assigned solutions engineer.

What hardware does SkyNav support?
SkyNav officially supports the DJI Matrice 300, the Skydio X2, and any
PX4-based flight controller running firmware 1.13 or later. Support for
ArduPilot-based systems is in closed beta as of this FAQ's publication.

How accurate is the indoor positioning?
Under standard test conditions (well-lit warehouse, moderate texture on
surfaces), SkyNav achieves positioning accuracy within 8 centimeters.
Accuracy degrades in low-light or texture-poor environments (e.g. plain
white walls), where drift can reach 30-40 centimeters over a 100-meter
flight path without periodic re-localization.

Is there a free trial?
Yes. All new accounts get a 14-day trial of the Professional tier with a
hard cap of 2 drones. No credit card is required to start the trial.

What is the cancellation policy?
Subscriptions can be cancelled at any time from the billing portal.
Cancellation takes effect at the end of the current billing cycle; Aurora
Robotics does not offer prorated refunds for partial months.
