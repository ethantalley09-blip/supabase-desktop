Dana Reyes for State Senate — Field Operations Staff Reference (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)
Internal reference for canvassing and volunteer staff

NOTE: This document is fictional example content written for demo/testing
purposes, showing how the same chatbot that answers donor questions can
also serve as an internal staff copilot when the indexed documents cover
campaign operations, not just donor-facing content.

Canvassing shifts run in two blocks: 10am-1pm and 4pm-7pm, Tuesday
through Saturday. Volunteers should check in at the field office 15
minutes before their shift to get a walk packet and a working phone with
the canvassing app installed.

Door-knocking script order: introduce yourself and the campaign, ask the
household's top issue, note it in the app even if they don't want to talk
further, and only pivot to the candidate's position after they've spoken
first. Never argue with a hostile respondent — thank them for their time
and move to the next door.

Data entry rules: every door gets a result code (not home / refused /
supporter / undecided / opposed) even if no conversation happened. Blank
entries make the next canvasser re-knock a door that was already covered.

Volunteer safety: canvassers work in pairs after 6pm and in any
precinct flagged "low-lighting" in the walk list. Any canvasser who feels
unsafe at a specific address should mark it "skip — safety" in the app
and move on; a field captain follows up, never the canvasser alone.

Weekly team meeting is Thursdays at 6:30pm at the field office — covers
the week's door-knocking numbers, any script updates, and a rotating
"door story of the week" from the canvass notes.

New volunteer onboarding: a 30-minute training session covers the script,
the app, and safety rules above, required before a first solo shift; a
volunteer's first two shifts are always paired with an experienced
canvasser.
