Dana Reyes for State Senate — Contribution Limit Policy (FICTIONAL EXAMPLE)
Effective January 2026

NOTE: This document is a fictional example written for demo/testing
purposes only. It does not reflect real FEC, state, or any other
jurisdiction's actual contribution limits. Any real campaign must confirm
current limits with qualified legal counsel before relying on them.

Individual contributors may give up to $3,300 per election to this
campaign committee (primary and general elections are counted as separate
elections, so an individual may give up to $3,300 for the primary and a
further $3,300 for the general election in the same cycle).

Contributions from an individual's employer are not counted toward that
individual's limit — the limit applies to the person, not the household
or employer.

Contributions from federal political action committees (PACs) are capped
at $5,000 per election.

Corporate contributions directly from a corporate treasury are prohibited
entirely.

Any single contribution of $200 or more requires the donor's occupation
and employer to be recorded before the contribution can be accepted.

Contributions that would put a donor over the applicable limit must be
either refunded in full or redesignated to a different election (with the
donor's written permission) within 60 days of the campaign becoming aware
of the excess.

An informal "affiliated committee" or shared-employer cluster of donors
approaching the limit together is a pattern the treasurer should review,
but campaign staff may not make a final legal determination that such a
cluster is an illegal affiliated-entity contribution — that determination
requires counsel.
