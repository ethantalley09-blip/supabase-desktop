Aurora Robotics — Remote Work & Time Off Policy
Effective January 2026

1. Remote Work Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their 90-day probationary period
are eligible to work remotely up to 3 days per week. Employees on the
Hardware Test team must be on-site at least 4 days per week due to lab
equipment access requirements.

2. Paid Time Off (PTO)
Full-time employees accrue 15 PTO days per year during their first two
years of employment, increasing to 20 days per year starting in year three.
PTO accrues monthly and can be carried over up to a maximum of 10 unused
days into the following calendar year. Unused PTO beyond that cap is
forfeited on December 31st.

3. Sick Leave
Sick leave is separate from PTO. Employees receive 8 paid sick days per
year, which do not carry over and are not paid out upon termination.

4. Home Office Stipend
Remote-eligible employees receive a one-time $750 home office stipend
during their first 60 days, plus an ongoing $40/month stipend for internet
and phone expenses. The stipend must be requested through the Expensify
portal under category "Remote Equipment."

5. Core Collaboration Hours
Regardless of work location, all employees are expected to be available
for meetings between 10:00 AM and 3:00 PM in their local time zone,
Monday through Thursday. Fridays have no mandated core hours.

6. Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $100 do not require pre-approval and are
reimbursed within 5 business days of submission. Expenses over $100
require manager approval before the expense is incurred.
